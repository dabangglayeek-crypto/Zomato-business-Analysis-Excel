# Zomato Business Analysis Project

The Zomato Business Analysis Project explores restaurant data to understand restaurant distribution, customer engagement, ratings, pricing, and cuisine availability across cities and countries. The objective was to convert raw data into business insights and present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse restaurant distribution across locations.
- Examine ratings and customer voting patterns.
- Compare average restaurant costs.
- Explore cuisine availability.
- Present findings through clear visualisations.

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

I reviewed and prepared the dataset before analysis. The process included checking data types, identifying missing or inconsistent values, and validating important fields such as restaurant ID, country, city, cuisines, ratings, votes, and average cost. Records with missing cuisine information were removed to improve data consistency and reliability.

## Tools and Techniques

Microsoft Excel was the primary tool used for this project. I applied Excel formulas and functions to organise and analyse information. PivotTables helped summarise data across different categories, while PivotCharts and visualisations made comparisons easier to understand.

I developed an interactive dashboard combining key performance indicators and charts in one view. Slicers for year, country, and city allowed users to filter results and explore selected locations and time periods. This made the report easier to navigate and supported flexible analysis.

## Analysis Performed

The analysis covered restaurant counts, average ratings, votes, average cost, geographic distribution, and cuisine information. Comparing these measures helped identify differences in restaurant presence, customer engagement, and pricing across markets. The dashboard enabled users to explore these metrics without manually creating separate reports.

## Project Outcome

This project provided practical experience in an end-to-end business analysis workflow, from preparing raw data to presenting findings in an interactive dashboard. It strengthened my ability to organise datasets, analyse business metrics, identify patterns, and communicate results visually. The final report demonstrates how Excel can transform restaurant data into structured insights that support business understanding and informed decision-making.
